Project Overview

About the Project & Technical Methodology

AI-Driven Early Anemia Risk Prediction and Personalized Nutrition Recommendation System for Women.

Problem Statement & Motivation

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), anemia affects approximately 29.9% of women of reproductive age (15–49 years) worldwide, and nearly 36.5% of pregnant women. In low- and middle-income regions, dietary iron deficiency is responsible for more than 50% of all cases.

Conventional detection depends on laboratory blood draws (e.g., hemoglobin concentration via CBC, serum ferritin levels). However, logistical hurdles, clinical costs, and lack of symptom awareness often prevent timely testing. This academic project develops a non-invasive, early AI risk-screening prototype combined with food-first personalized nutritional guidance to empower women with actionable insights before severe deficiency occurs.

Machine Learning Pipeline Workflow
  1. Feature Ingestion: Ingests 17 distinct biological, dietary, and symptomatic variables.
  2. Preprocessing: Numerical features (Age, Height, Weight, BMI) are standardized via StandardScaler; categorical features (food intake frequencies) are one-hot encoded via OneHotEncoder; binary physiological indicators are passed through.
  3. Model Comparison: Evaluated Logistic Regression, Decision Tree Classifier, and Random Forest Classifier across Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and ROC-AUC.
  4. Model Selection: Random Forest Classifier selected for superior non-linear feature interaction modeling and generalization on multi-factorial risk data.
  5. Probability Mapping: Employs prototype probability thresholds (Low: < 0.35, Moderate: 0.35–0.69, High: ≥ 0.70) for early lifestyle alerting.
Mandatory Academic & Clinical Boundary Notice

1. Not a Diagnostic System: This application is strictly an educational research prototype. Anemia is a clinical condition requiring blood testing (hemoglobin, hematocrit, ferritin, iron binding capacity) evaluated by certified clinicians.

2. No Medication or Supplement Prescriptions: High-dose iron supplementation without laboratory confirmation carries severe risks, including hemochromatosis and organ toxicity. This system adheres strictly to food-first educational recommendations.

3. Data Privacy & Protection: All user records, assessments, and biometric data are secured with bcrypt/werkzeug password hashing and strict database relational isolation.
